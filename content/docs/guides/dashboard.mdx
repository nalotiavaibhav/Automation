The dashboard is the front door of Flowmax. It surfaces five things in roughly one screen:

## 1. KPI Strip

Four headline numbers across the top:

- **Total calls** — count of all calls in the selected window
- **Bookings made** — calls that ended with `outcome === 'booked'`
- **Missed calls recovered** — calls Flowmax answered when no human was available (often the bulk of value)
- **Revenue impact** — a rough estimate based on bookings × average ticket size

Each card shows a 7-day trend arrow (% change vs prior period).

## 2. Calls Chart

A 14-day chart of call volume by day, segmented by outcome. Hover any bar for the per-day breakdown.

## 3. Filtering Toolbar

Above the call log:

- **Date range** — last 24h / 7d / 30d / 90d / all
- **Outcome filter** — booked / info / missed / follow-up
- **Urgency filter** — emergency / urgent / routine
- **Search** — free-text against caller name, phone, summary

Filters compose. They do not persist across page reloads in v1.

## 4. Call Log Table

Excel-like sortable table. Click any header to sort; click a row to open the detail sheet.

Emergency rows are highlighted with a red left-border so they're impossible to miss when scanning.

## 5. Call Detail Sheet

Slides in from the right when you click a row. Shows phone, duration, cost, outcome, urgency, recording, summary, full transcript, and (for emergencies) the **Owner Alert** status — did the SMS land?

## What the dashboard does NOT show

- Per-customer billing or usage metering (multi-tenancy isn't built yet)
- Real-time live transcripts during a call (post-call only)
- Charts segmented by AI assistant — only one assistant per business in v1
