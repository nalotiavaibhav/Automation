## Contacts

The contacts page is a list of every person Flowmax has spoken with. Each row shows:

- Name (best-effort from the call)
- Phone
- Email (if captured)
- Service of interest
- Status (`new` / `contacted` / `qualified` / `booked` / `completed`)
- Urgency
- Source (`inbound_call` / `missed_call` / `website` / `referral`)
- Last contacted (relative time)

Click a row to see the contact detail with their full call history, every transcript, and the linked CRM record.

## Pipeline (kanban)

Open `/pipeline`. You'll see a kanban board with columns:

1. **New** — fresh inbound that needs a human touch
2. **Contacted** — first follow-up done
3. **Qualified** — confirmed they want the service
4. **Booked** — appointment scheduled
5. **Completed** — service done, ready to close

Drag a card across stages. The change syncs to your connected CRM within ~5 seconds.

## How contacts get there

Every call upserts the caller's phone into the contacts table. The first call from a new phone creates a contact at `status: 'new'`; subsequent calls update the same record.

If a CRM is connected, the contact is mirrored there with the right stage mapping (see your integration's doc for the exact mapping).

## What's not here yet

- **Bulk actions** — can't multi-select and bulk-move cards
- **Custom stages** — the 5 stages are hardcoded
- **Per-rep assignment** — every contact is "yours" because the app is single-business in v1
- **Lead source attribution** — `source` is captured but no analytics view exists yet
