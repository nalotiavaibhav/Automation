## What this does

When Vapi classifies a call as `urgency === 'emergency'` in its end-of-call structured output, Flowmax immediately sends an SMS to your phone via Twilio with:

- The caller's phone number
- The AI's one-line summary of the situation
- The local time the call ended
- A deep link to the call detail page (transcript + recording)

Example:

```
EMERGENCY CALL - Flowmax
From: +1 555 123 4567
Summary: Burst pipe, basement flooding, needs tech within 2hrs.
4:32 PM 05/12
Open: https://flowmax.app/calls/abc123
```

Two SMS segments. Plain text, no emoji, GSM-7. Costs about $0.027 per alert delivered.

## Architecture

1. Vapi ends the call → POSTs `end-of-call-report` to `/api/webhooks/vapi`
2. `vapi-extractor.ts` parses the payload, extracts `urgency`
3. `sync-orchestrator.ts` runs as fire-and-forget. **Step 0** (before any CRM work) checks `urgency === 'emergency'` and fires the SMS
4. `sendEmergencySms()` in `lib/notifications/sms.ts` calls the Twilio API, logs the result, never throws
5. The Owner Alert section in the call detail sheet queries `/api/alerts?callId=...` which scans recent Twilio messages for a body containing the call's deep link

Critically: **the SMS fires BEFORE the CRM sync**. So even if HubSpot/Zoho is down, the emergency reaches you.

## Setup

### 1. Twilio credentials

Create a Twilio account at https://twilio.com. Buy or use a test number. Note the Account SID + Auth Token.

```
TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID=AC...
TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN=...
TWILIO_FROM_PHONE=+15551234567
OWNER_ALERT_PHONE=+15559998888   # your phone
OWNER_TIMEZONE=America/New_York
NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://flowmax.app
```

### 2. A2P 10DLC registration (US production only)

US carriers (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon) throttle business SMS 30–70% if you're not registered with The Campaign Registry. Without registration, emergency alerts will land late or not at all.

Steps:

1. **Brand registration** — Twilio Console → Messaging → Regulatory Compliance → A2P 10DLC. Pick **Low Volume Standard** ($4.50 one-time, $4/mo). Fill in business legal name, EIN, address.
2. **Campaign registration** — Use case: **Account Notifications**. Sample message: paste the template above. Include "Reply STOP to opt out" in your help docs. ($15 one-time vet, $1.50/mo)
3. **Attach phone number** to the approved campaign.
4. Wait 1–3 business days for vetting.

International? Skip this section and consider WhatsApp Business instead (see below).

### 3. Vapi prompt tuning

The AI must reliably mark emergencies. In your Vapi assistant prompt, include explicit examples:

> Mark `urgency = "emergency"` when the caller describes any of: active flooding, burst or gushing water, gas smell, fire, sparks, no heat in winter, no AC in extreme heat, smoke, sewage backup, danger to life or property.
>
> Mark `urgency = "urgent"` for: same-day requests, before-guests-arrive, business closures imminent.
>
> Everything else is `urgency = "routine"`.

Test with a phone call simulating each scenario. Verify the urgency in the end-of-call-report payload.

## Testing locally

Twilio offers test credentials that don't actually send SMS but exercise the full API:

- `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID=AC<test-sid>` (from Twilio Console → Keys & Credentials)
- `TWILIO_FROM_PHONE=+15005550006` (magic-always-succeeds)
- `OWNER_ALERT_PHONE=+15005550001` (will return InvalidNumber, useful for error-path testing)

For end-to-end testing, swap to live credentials and your own cell, place a real Vapi call, and have the AI mark it emergency.

## What's intentionally NOT here

- **ACK loop** — no "reply YES to acknowledge". Fire-and-forget by design.
- **Retry** — Twilio handles carrier-level retries; we don't layer our own.
- **Escalation** — single recipient only. Multi-step escalation (owner → manager → on-call) is a future project.
- **Quiet hours** — emergencies always send, regardless of time.
- **Custom recipients per emergency type** — one phone, all emergencies.

If you need any of these, file an issue.

## WhatsApp instead of SMS

For non-US markets where WhatsApp is the default messenger (India, Brazil, Mexico, parts of EU/Africa), WhatsApp Business via Twilio is 3–5× cheaper than US SMS and supports interactive buttons.

WhatsApp setup is its own project — currently not wired in v1. If you're in a WhatsApp-heavy market, please tell us; it informs prioritization.
