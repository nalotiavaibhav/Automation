## What does Flowmax cost?

Pricing is published at https://flowmax.app/pricing. Current tiers:

- **Starter** — $49/mo · 200 included voice minutes · all CRM integrations · emergency SMS · email support
- **Pro** — $149/mo · 700 included voice minutes · 3 assistants · priority email · cheaper overage
- **Enterprise** — Custom · unlimited minutes · HIPAA BAA · dedicated support · custom voice cloning

Annual prepay is 20% off either paid tier.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days, 50 minutes, no credit card required. After 14 days, the AI stops answering until you pick a plan.

## What does "voice minute" mean?

Every second Vapi's AI is on a call counts toward your monthly quota. Silent hold periods count. Dropped/redirected calls count for the seconds they were active.

## What happens if I exceed the included minutes?

Each tier has an overage rate:

- Starter: $0.30 per additional minute
- Pro: $0.25 per additional minute

Overage bills at the end of the month with the same card.

## Are CRM integrations limited by tier?

No — every tier includes HubSpot, Zoho, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber. Salesforce is on the roadmap.

The only tier-gating is on the **number of active integrations at once** and **API access** (Pro+).

## Is the emergency SMS feature counted in minutes?

No. Voice minutes and SMS messages are separate. SMS alerts are included free at fair-use volumes (~500 alerts/mo). Beyond that we charge at-cost.

## Do I pay for Twilio separately?

No — Twilio is included in the platform. You don't need your own Twilio account unless you want a custom sender number.

## What about my call recordings?

Stored for 90 days on Starter, 365 days on Pro, and indefinitely on Enterprise (or until you delete). Storage is included.

## Refunds?

If Flowmax fails to answer a call in your first month and we can't promptly fix it, you get a full refund. Email support and we'll handle it.

## Annual prepay?

20% off. Billed in advance. No prorated refund if you cancel mid-year — but you keep service through the end of the term.

## Does pricing change with usage?

The headline price doesn't move with usage — you keep paying the monthly subscription. Only the overage rate kicks in past your included quota.

## Multi-location pricing?

Each location is a separate Pro or Enterprise subscription today. Volume discounts kick in at 3+ locations — contact sales.
