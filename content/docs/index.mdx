Flowmax is the AI receptionist for service businesses — plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, restaurants, clinics — who can't afford to miss a call and can't justify a full-time front desk.

## What you get

- **Always-on phone answering.** Trained on your services, your address, your hours. Sounds human, books appointments, captures structured data.
- **One call → contact + deal + appointment in your CRM.** HubSpot, Zoho, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro out of the box.
- **Emergency SMS the moment something bad happens.** Burst pipe, gas smell, no heat in winter — your phone buzzes within seconds.
- **A dashboard that actually shows the call.** Transcript, recording, AI summary, urgency, outcome, and a one-click jump to the contact's CRM record.

## Who Flowmax is for

If you run a 1–20 person field-service company and you've ever:

- Lost a $4,000 emergency job because the call went to voicemail at 11 PM
- Hired a part-time front-desk person who couldn't quite stick the AI-style consistency you wanted
- Tried Google Voice or an answering service and watched it generate qualified-sounding leads with no detail you could actually act on

…Flowmax is built for you.
