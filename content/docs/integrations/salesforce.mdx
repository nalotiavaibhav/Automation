> **Coming soon.** A Salesforce adapter is on the roadmap but not yet shipping. We're prioritizing it once we hear it from two paying customers.

## Why not now

Salesforce's integration surface is large (Sales Cloud · Service Cloud · custom objects · Apex triggers). Doing it well is a 3–4 week project on its own, and most of our early SMB customers are on HubSpot or Zoho.

## What it will look like

When it ships, expect:

- OAuth 2.0 Connected App
- Lead upsert (or Contact, configurable)
- Opportunity creation with stage mapping
- Task / Event activities

## Workarounds in the meantime

- Use Zapier or Make to forward HubSpot events into Salesforce
- Build a custom integration on top of the `lib/crm/adapters/base.ts` interface — this is the same shape used by every other adapter
- Vote for Salesforce in [our roadmap](/docs/resources/changelog) (file an issue)
